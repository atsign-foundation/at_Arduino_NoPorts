Flash your device directly from the browser — no drivers or tools required.

⚠️ WebSerial is not supported in this browser. Use Chrome or Edge (desktop).

Instructions

  1. Select the board above that matches your hardware.
  2. Plug the device into this computer via USB.
  3. Click Connect & Flash, then choose the correct COM/serial port.
  4. Wait for the flash to complete (~30-60 s).
  5. CYD2USB S3 only: After flashing completes, press the physical RST button on the device once to reboot. The S3's native USB does not auto-reset after a web flash.
  6. Follow the on-screen setup wizard on the device display.
🔒 WebSerial requires a secure context. This page works on localhost automatically. To flash over a local network, front this container with HTTPS (e.g. Caddy, Nginx reverse proxy, or Tailscale Funnel).
